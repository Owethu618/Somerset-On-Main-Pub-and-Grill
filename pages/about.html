About Us

Background

Somerset On Main Pub and Grill is known for its delicious fresh food and its signature flame-grilled meat. Somerset On Main Pub and Grill was founded on 27 July 2009 by Mr Raulstone. A restaurant rooted in a beautiful town called Somerset East, now known as KwaNojoli. Somerset On Main Pub and Grill has been owned by Mr Raulstone’s daughter, Lauren Raulstone, for the past 3 years, and the restaurant is still going strong.
