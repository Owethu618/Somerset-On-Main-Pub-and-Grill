About Us

Background

Somerset On Main Pub and Grill is known for its delicious fresh food and its signature flame-grilled meat. Somerset On Main Pub and Grill was founded on 27 July 2009 by Mr Raulstone. A restaurant rooted in a beautiful town called Somerset East, now known as KwaNojoli. Somerset On Main Pub and Grill has been owned by Mr Raulstone’s daughter, Lauren Raulstone, for the past 3 years, and the restaurant is still going strong.



Mission:

• Committed to serving great food, creating a welcoming atmosphere, and continually improving one stride at a time.

Vision:

• To be the premier dining destination in Somerset East, known for our exceptional food, outstanding service, and memorable experiences. Continuing to serve different kinds of food filled with culture and the warmth of the community, and looking forward to welcoming familiar and new faces from around South Africa and around the world.
