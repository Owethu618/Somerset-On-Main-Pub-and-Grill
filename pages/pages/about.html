A FAMILY-OWNED LOCAL

Good things bring people together.

Founded on 27 July 2009 by Mr Raulstone, Somerset On Main Pub and Grill has grown alongside the beautiful town of Somerset East, now known as KwaNojoli.

Today, his daughter Lauren Raulstone carries the story forward. We’re proud to welcome neighbours, travellers and new friends for fresh food, signature flame-grilled meat and the easy-going community spirit that makes this place feel like home.
